Swivelcut

A compact cardboard-cutting system that makes low-fidelity prototyping safer, more accurate and more accessible in secondary school Design & Technology workshops. It grew out of research across two schools, where students told us cardboard modelling was slow, rough and risky with a craft knife. A fold-away arm with swappable heads: students trace a shape by hand, then the arm cuts it with an enclosed blade, so they don’t need any CAD.
